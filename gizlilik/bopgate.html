Privacy Policy (English)

Bopgate ("the game") is a single-player arcade game for Android, published under the package name com.cozulur.bopgate. This policy explains what data the game handles.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The developer does not run any server and does not receive, store or sell any personal data. The game has no accounts, no chat and no analytics of its own.

Data stored on your device

Your best score, star wallet, owned items, upgrades, daily quests, sound and language settings are stored locally on your device (browser/WebView storage). They never leave your device unless you use cloud save (below). Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Third-party services (Google)

ServicePurposeData it may process
Google AdMobOptional rewarded ads (free continue, double stars). No ads are shown unless you tap to watch one. Advertising ID, device and app information, approximate location from IP, ad interactions
Google Play BillingOne-time in-app purchasesPurchase information, handled by Google Play; the developer never sees payment details
Google Play Games ServicesLeaderboard and cloud saveYour Play Games profile, scores, the saved game file

These services are operated by Google and follow Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Ads are optional and only appear when you choose to watch one. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Leaderboard and cloud save are only used if you sign in to Google Play Games. You can sign out or delete your Play Games data in the Play Games app.
  • The "No Ads" purchase lets you get ad rewards without watching ads.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Questions: bopgate.destek@gmail.com

Gizlilik Politikası (Türkçe)

Bopgate ("oyun"), com.cozulur.bopgate paket adıyla yayımlanan tek oyunculu bir Android arcade oyunudur. Bu metin oyunun hangi verileri işlediğini açıklar.

Geliştiricinin topladığı veriler

Hiçbiri. Geliştirici bir sunucu çalıştırmaz; kişisel veri almaz, saklamaz, satmaz. Oyunda hesap, sohbet ya da oyunun kendine ait bir analitik sistemi yoktur.

Cihazında saklanan veriler

Rekorun, yıldız cüzdanın, sahip olduğun ürünler, güçlendirmeler, günlük görevler, ses ve dil ayarların cihazında yerel olarak saklanır. Bulut kaydı (aşağıda) kullanmadıkça cihazından çıkmaz. Oyunu kaldırınca silinir.
